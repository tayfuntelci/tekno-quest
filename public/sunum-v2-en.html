LEVEL 8
🧠 WHY IS IT HARD TO PUT THE PHONE DOWN?
PAGE 1/6
It's not your fault — it's about how your brain works!
🧠

WHAT'S HAPPENING INSIDE YOUR BRAIN?

In your brain there's a happiness chemical called dopamine. When something nice happens, your brain releases dopamine and you say, "Ooh, I liked that!"

In the old days, to get dopamine you had to pick fruit, play games or make friends. It took time and a little effort.

Now? You scroll TikTok and there's a new video every 10 seconds. With each new video your brain gives a little burst of dopamine. Your brain says: "Wow, this is so easy! Keep going!"

That's exactly why it's hard to put the phone down. The screen trains your brain to expect very fast happiness.
⚠️ HERE'S THE PROBLEM:
Once your brain gets used to fast dopamine, the slower things (studying, reading a book, doing sports) start to feel boring. That's because they give dopamine slowly.
🎯 THE FIX: THE 12-MINUTE RULE
Even if something feels boring (homework, a book, sport) — just try it for 12 minutes. After the first 12 minutes your brain starts to say "oh, this isn't so bad after all." The dopamine comes slowly, but it comes to stay!
LEVEL 8
🧩 WHAT SHAPES YOUR BRAIN?
PAGE 2/6
Whatever you do, your brain grows stronger in that direction.
🧠

YOUR BRAIN IS LIKE A MUSCLE

Your brain has 86 billion cells. These cells are joined to each other by thin pathways. The more you do something, the thicker and stronger the pathways used for that task become.

When you're learning to ride a bike you struggle and fall. Then your brain strengthens the "bicycle pathway" and you start riding without even thinking. Times tables, chess, a musical instrument, a foreign language — they all work the same way.

So in the end: your brain is shaped by whatever you do. So what are you doing? That's the really important question.
🤖 EXAMPLE: DOING HOMEWORK WITH AI
Let's say the teacher gives you homework: "Write 3 important events from Atatürk's life."

🟢 WAY 1 — By thinking: You open your book. You read it. You picture the events in your mind. You think, "Which one is more important?" The homework takes 20 minutes, but your brain's thinking pathway grows stronger. Even a week later, you still remember those events.

🔴 WAY 2 — You ask the AI: You type the question into an AI tool and the answer comes in 5 seconds. You copy it, paste it, and the homework is done. But your brain didn't work at all. If someone asks you the next day which 3 events they were, you can't remember.

Both reach the right answer. But one improves you, the other improves the AI.
📱

IF YOU USE TOO MUCH INTERNET/SCREEN

Scientists have studied children's brains using MRI machines. In children with more than 7 hours of screen time a day, they noticed these signs:
  • 🧠 The deep-thinking area (the cortex) was found to be thinner.
  • 📖 The connections in the reading and language areas were seen to weaken.
  • ⏱️ Focusing for a long time became harder, because the brain gets used to short videos.
  • 💭 The ability to imagine fades, because the screen serves up every image ready-made.
  • 🤖 In children who rely on AI tools, the ability to think for themselves was found to weaken.
  • 😴 Blue light disturbs sleep, so the brain can't refresh itself well enough.
Your brain gets shaped "to fit the screen" — it gets used to fast, shallow, ready-made thinking.
✋

IF YOU USE YOUR HANDS

An interesting fact: your hands train your brain. The nerves in your fingers are connected to the largest area of the brain. That's why every activity that uses your hands makes your brain stronger:
  • 🧱 Lego, puzzles: Spatial thinking, planning and patience
  • ✏️ Drawing, painting: Imagination and fine coordination
  • 🎹 Musical instrument: Two hands + reading notes + rhythm — all parts of the brain work at once
  • 🥧 Cooking, kneading dough: Putting things in order, measuring and touch memory
  • 📚 Reading books: The events come alive in your mind — your brain builds the scene on its own
  • ⚽ Sports, dance: Body-brain coordination and self-control
  • 🪡 Origami, crafts: Concentration, fine movements and patience
Your brain gets shaped "to fit real life" — it gains a deep, flexible and creative way of thinking.
🌟 WHAT DOES IT MEAN TO BE SMART?
Being smart doesn't mean "knowing everything." Because nowadays AI knows everything too.

Real intelligence is this: asking the right question, connecting ideas, creating new things, breaking big problems into pieces, and working with patience. These are things AI can't do — and they only grow when you use your own brain.

AI is a helpful tool. But you should think first, then ask it. If you fire off a question without thinking, you're the one who falls behind.
💡 THE BOTTOM LINE: TECHNOLOGY IS NOT THE ENEMY — BALANCE IS KEY
Phones, AI, the internet — they're all wonderful tools. But on their own they don't make you better. In fact, if you overuse them, they can weaken your abilities.

Here's what matters: Use the screen, but don't forget your hands. Do your homework, but think for yourself first. Ask questions, but research on your own first. That's the only way your brain grows stronger.

DIGITAL SMARTS — DON'T BELIEVE EVERYTHING YOU SEE!

You saw a video online: a famous actor saying something strange. Is it real? Maybe not.
🎭 WHAT IS A DEEPFAKE?
AI can copy a person's face and voice. That way, videos are made that look like the person is saying things they never said. These are called deepfakes — it means "deep fake."

Example: a video where a celebrity says "send me this money and I'll give you back 10 times more." In reality that celebrity never said it — AI copied their face and voice. Many people were fooled.
🤖 AI IMAGES LOOK VERY REAL TOO
You saw a photo: a giant octopus wrapping around a ship. Is it real? No — AIs like ChatGPT or Midjourney can make this kind of picture in 5 seconds. The rule "a photo = the truth" no longer holds.
  • 🔍 The 3-source rule: Saw a piece of news? Check it with 2-3 other trustworthy places too (like BBC, NTV, TRT)
  • 🎥 Is a video suspicious? Check the celebrity's official account — if it isn't posted there, it could be fake
  • 👀 Warning signs: Does the light on the face look odd? Does the mouth match the words exactly? Do the eyes look natural?
  • 🚨 Any message asking for money, passwords or information is suspicious — ask your family!
  • 🎭 Social media is everyone's best moments — not real life
  • ❤️ Number of followers ≠ Happiness
